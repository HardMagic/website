hollywood

A material. A myth. A city of dreams.

Holly wood.
Hollywood.

Holly—the evergreen tree—has carried associations of winter endurance, returning spring, myth, and magic in European traditions. Its wood belongs to a timeless material imagination: nature made symbolic, meaning made tangible.

The city of Hollywood arrived at its name by another history, not from a magician’s wand. Yet the accidental poetry is perfect. Split the word in the imagination—holly wood / Hollywood—and folklore meets a city whose images travel the world, alter consciousness, and make millions of people believe in realities that did not exist before the lights came up.

That threshold is where HardMagic works. Not illusion instead of truth, but imagination with enough craft, strategy, and force to become culture.

The folklore and the place-name are distinct histories. Sources: National Trust on holly folklore ↗ · Los Angeles City Planning on Hollywood’s name ↗
